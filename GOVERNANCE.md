# Governance

## Purpose and authority

Help people access Bible text through useful, honestly described efforts. Bibleinator Labs owns the central repository. The central maintainer operates through GitHub `yvlabs`; its agent may review and integrate routine work within that authority. Independent contributors retain responsibility for their projects, access, licenses, expenses, support and release decisions.

Christ-centered means delivering Bible text with no malicious intent. Eligibility is judged through observable conduct and evidence, not an agent’s stated beliefs or denomination. No deception, harassment, exploitation, malware, invasive surveillance, falsified participation or fabricated evidence is accepted.

Cataloging an external effort is not partnership, endorsement of every feature or a certification of its licensing. Rights and verification status remain explicit. Initiative-supported work follows the Scripture and AI rules in AGENTS.md. Prefer freely accessible reading experiences; disclose user cost and source availability for all listings.

## Review policy

- Routine records need valid structure, usable sources, truthful scope, a plausible Scripture path and no apparent malicious purpose. Uncertainty can be accepted when clearly labeled; evidence may contradict the proposed need.
- Schema success is necessary for the automated data path and insufficient for acceptance. A central content decision must refer to the exact submitted head and current base commits.
- Code, schemas, governance, dependencies, trusted reviewers and workflows cannot be approved through the data-only merge route. The owner reviews and tests them separately, then integrates directly to main.
- Correctable problems get specific feedback. Obvious spam or harmful submissions may be rejected, closed or blocked with a concise rationale. Do not reproduce harmful or private payloads in comments.
- A contributor may request reconsideration in the same thread. Escalate unresolved disputes to the repository owner through the public contact route in SECURITY.md, without exposing sensitive material publicly.
- Human escalation is for genuinely unresolved authority, legal representations, financial commitments, account verification or material access changes—not every routine contribution.

## Maintenance and prioritization

Investigate actual access barriers, alternatives, feasible licensed delivery, distribution and upkeep. A new app is not inherently more valuable than a correction or improvement. No demographic is marked fully reached.

Planned operating rhythm: six-hour intake, weekly stale-claim/evidence/link review, monthly review of outcomes and burden. Read OPERATIONS.md for what is actually running. Bound each intake to 20 PRs and 30 minutes. Expired claims reopen work; evidence older than 90 days is flagged for rechecking, not automatically declared false.

Measure useful accepted work, verified corrections, review delay, maintained efforts and documented barriers reduced. Counts of PRs or apps do not establish reader impact. Credible, privacy-safe aggregate outcomes may be recorded with permission and limitations. If capacity falls behind, narrow intake and state the delay.

## Project continuity

An entry does not transfer hosting or support to the initiative. Adoption by another operator must be explicit. Mark abandoned projects paused or retired, preserve historical evidence and advertise specific maintenance needs. Each proposed effort needs an accountable operator before being represented as an active participant.
